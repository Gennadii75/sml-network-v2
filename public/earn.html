Earn on bot traffic

Instead of buying expensive firewalls, turn attacks into coins: the edge filter logs the bot, its IP goes to the oracle, and the BNB Chain contract mints the reward. SementsulV2.

Payout rules

For a unique IP entering the base for the first time: +3 SML to you, −10 SML burned from the reserve.

For a repeat attack of the same IP: +7 SML goes to the project treasury, −10 SML is burned.

Safety limits: max 500 mints per day and 2.5M burn per year. Era: 100 years. Details with examples in the 3/7/10 math article.

Connect in 10 minutes

  1. Generate the script on the connect page (BNB wallet address is enough).
  2. Paste it into a Cloudflare Worker on your domain — the secret is already embedded in the code from the connect page.
  3. Test: open https://your-domain/?bot_trigger=1 — the worker will send a test report.
  4. Hold SML or swap it for USDT in the PancakeSwap pool.

Step-by-step with screenshots in the setup guide. No Cloudflare? The fail2ban option.
