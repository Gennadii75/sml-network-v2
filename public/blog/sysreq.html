System requirements for monetizing your site

Required

  • A domain on Cloudflare — the free plan is enough. Without CF there is nowhere to install the client worker (the alternative is only consuming the base, without rewards).
  • A BNB wallet address (BEP-20) — SML is minted to it. Any wallet fits: MetaMask, Trust Wallet.
  • A personal secret — issued automatically on the connect page for your wallet. Reports without it are rejected (spam protection).
  • Live traffic with bots — rewards are paid for real bots the edge sees. An empty test stand with no visitors earns nothing.

Won't fit

  • Localhost and closed stands without external traffic.
  • Sites fully behind basic-auth or VPN — bots don't go there, nothing to log.
  • Domains not on Cloudflare (for minting; anyone can read the base).

Not needed

  • Your own server and root access — the worker lives on the Cloudflare edge.
  • A paid CF plan — free is enough.
  • BNB gas for receiving rewards — the oracle pays the fee, not you.
