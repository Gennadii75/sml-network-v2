How to tell bots from real visitors

The network worker relies on the Cloudflare score, but every webmaster should understand the signals.

1. Bot Score

request.cf.botManagement.score — from 1 (definitely a bot) to 99 (definitely human). The SML network threshold: inverted threat above 10 counts as a bot. The legacy threatScore field is deprecated but kept as a fallback.

2. Quick bot signs

SignalBotHuman
JavaScript / cookiesoften missingpresent
ASNdatacenterhome ISP
User-Agentempty, curl, pythonbrowser-like
Request rhythmsteady, dozens per seconduneven
TLS fingerprint (JA3)library-likebrowser-like

3. Why not ban manually

Manual bans on a single signal hit real users (mobile networks, corporate proxies). The SML worker blocks nobody — it only logs IPs. Blocking stays conscious, via the blocklist.
