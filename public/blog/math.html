Example

A site catching 100 unique bots a day earns you 300 SML/day while 1000 SML/day burns from the reserve. Repeat offenders additionally burn the reserve — scarcity grows. Live numbers are on Stats.

Limits (anti-farming)

  • Max 500 mints per day network-wide — sending fake IPs is pointless.
  • Max 2.5M burn per year — the era spans 100 years.
  • Paid only for IPs the edge actually saw (oracle check against D1).

Honestly: dollar income depends on the SML price and pool liquidity. Estimate conservatively.
