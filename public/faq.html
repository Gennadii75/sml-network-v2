Frequently asked questions

What is SML?

SML (Sementsul) is a deflationary BEP-20 token on BNB Chain. Supply 1B; 250M sits in the burn reserve on the contract balance. V2 contract: 0x1FE40AAd7c3c3ccEB4efc9099EFBC57D2Dcc0dC7.

How to earn SML on bot traffic?

Install a Cloudflare Worker with the network script on your site. The worker logs bots and reports them to the oracle. Every unique IP entering the base for the first time mints +3 SML to your wallet.

Script generator · Details

How much is burned per mint?

10 SML from the reserve per mint: 10 burn + 3 reward (unique IP), 10 burn + 7 to treasury (repeat attack). The net is always deflationary: −7 and −3.

The math

Does the worker block real visitors?

No. Only IP logging and traffic passthrough. No captchas, no 403.

False positives

What is needed to connect?

A domain on Cloudflare (free is enough), a BNB wallet address. The personal secret is issued automatically by the connect page — paste it into the worker as is.

Connect page · 10-minute guide

When does the reward arrive?

The oracle runs in ~15 minute cycles: reads D1 → calls the contract → marks processed rows. The first mint usually arrives within 15–30 minutes after an attack.

What are the 500/day and 2.5M/year limits?

Anti-spam: max 500 mints per day network-wide and max 2.5M burn per year. Era: 100 years. Sending fake IPs is pointless.

How is V2 different from V1?

V2 is a new contract with honest burning (the reserve actually sits on its balance). The old pool does not migrate: liquidity is created from scratch under the V2 address.

Where to see network stats?

On the telemetry page: IPs in base, total burned, reserve remainder, contract year. Updated by the oracle.

How to use the base without Cloudflare?

Take blocklist.txt (one IP per line) into ipset/iptables or fail2ban via cron.
